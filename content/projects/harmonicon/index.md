A rhythm game for blues harmonica, diatonic and chromatic, built in Rust with
the Bevy engine. You play a real harmonica into a microphone and the game
detects the pitches in real time; notes scroll down the screen and you match
them on the instrument. It has a lesson curriculum, a jam session mode and an
in-game song editor.
