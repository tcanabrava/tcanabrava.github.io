Rocs is a graph theory IDE from KDE's education suite. You build a graph by
hand or generate one, then run algorithms over it in a built-in scripting
engine and watch what they do to the structure. It's aimed at students and
teachers working through graph theory, where being able to see an algorithm
move across a graph beats reading its pseudocode.
